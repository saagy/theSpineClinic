# Patient workspace correction audit

User review, 9 September 2026. No skills used. The preceding pilot is rejected.

1. Financial due information distracts doctors; remove it and its requests from their overview.
2. Image.network treats private storage keys as public URLs; use authenticated repository bytes.
3. Unbounded white regions and inconsistent column alignment flatten the hierarchy.
4. Default text-only popups do not match the icon-led schedule menus.
5. Empty-patient checks omit programs/history and privileged roles bypass them.
6. Spinners and abrupt section replacement do not resemble the loaded content.
7. Custom appointment rows discard schedule row behavior and shared status controls.
8. Inactive programs must be collapsed below active programs. Past/cancelled appointments
   also need a separately expandable history, rather than dominating upcoming visits.
9. Sorting and filters are separate controls with inconsistent sheets.
10. Add actions vary between quiet links, outlined buttons and unrelated header buttons.
11. Patient editing belongs in the app bar, not duplicated in the identity header.
12. The oversized linked-appointment footer competes with the note itself.
13. Flattening program documents loses the existing folder/gallery workflow.

Correction: bounded section panels, consistent header bands and aligned content in
the current theme; active clinical programs lead. Keep patient identity compact.
Use one prominent section add action, icon-led menus, unified filter/sort sheets,
matching skeletons and reduced-motion-aware transitions. Reception's outstanding
amount uses the theme's attention color. Preserve existing gallery and schedule
components and refresh contracts. Verify behavior with storage-key fixtures,
all roles, empty/nonempty patients, archived records, and narrow windows.

## September 9 follow-up audit
- Past records are unnecessarily concealed and date headings interrupt scanning.
- Nested tab cards reduce appointment width, especially on phones.
- Identity lacks an avatar; registration date occupies prominent header space.
- Attending staff lack the avatar treatment already used in Schedule.
- Document previews duplicate a dedicated tab without supporting overview decisions.
- Separate card outlines exaggerate unequal content heights in paired overview sections.
Revision: flat section toolbars/dividers throughout, full-width history with per-row dates,
header avatar and phone, staff avatars, clinical/visit/balance overview without document previews.
