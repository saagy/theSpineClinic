# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Clinic receptionists, doctors, and administrators using a phone-first interface during active patient-care and operational workflows. They need fast, clear actions with minimal training and strong role awareness.

## Product Purpose

Manage patients, appointments, clinical records, documents, payments, packages, and staff workflows in one secure system. Success means staff can complete routine clinic work quickly, confidently, and without losing context.

## Brand Personality

Calm, trustworthy, and efficient. The product should feel clinically professional and contemporary without becoming decorative or unfamiliar.

## Anti-references

Inconsistent component shapes, desktop-first controls, decorative motion, dense enterprise dashboards, generic AI styling, and interfaces that hide routine actions behind unfamiliar interaction patterns.

## Design Principles

- Make the next safe action obvious.
- Keep one visual and interaction vocabulary across every role.
- Prefer familiar mobile controls over novel patterns.
- Use hierarchy and spacing to reduce cognitive load.
- Preserve clarity across loading, error, empty, and data states.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use touch targets of at least 44 px, readable contrast, visible focus and disabled states, plain language, theme-aware colors, and reduced-motion-friendly feedback. Optimize for phone use and staff with varied technical and English proficiency.
