# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Clinic receptionists, doctors, and administrators using both PCs and phones during active patient-care and operational workflows. Both are primary usage contexts. They need fast, clear actions with minimal training and strong role awareness. Patients are managed records; the app currently has no patient login.

## Product Purpose

Manage patients, appointments, clinical records, documents, payments, packages, and staff workflows in one secure system. Success means staff can complete routine clinic work quickly, confidently, and without losing context.

## Brand Personality

Calm, trustworthy, and efficient. The product should feel clinically professional and contemporary without becoming decorative or unfamiliar.

## Anti-references

Mobile layouts stretched across desktop windows, controls that work only with a mouse, unnecessary cards and nested containers, decorative motion, weak information hierarchy, and interfaces that hide routine actions behind unfamiliar interaction patterns. Useful information density and familiar desktop controls are welcome when they support the task.

## Design Principles

- Make the next safe action obvious.
- Keep one visual and interaction vocabulary across roles while preserving their different permissions and tasks.
- Adapt navigation, content structure, forms, and action placement to available window space and input methods.
- Use hierarchy, alignment, and deliberate density to make records easy to scan.
- Choose rows, tables, cards, panels, or dedicated pages according to the task; no component is mandatory everywhere.
- Preserve useful context when moving between a list, a record, and an action.
- Preserve clarity across loading, error, empty, and data states.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use touch targets of at least 44 logical pixels, readable contrast, visible keyboard focus and disabled states, plain language, theme-aware colors, and reduced-motion-friendly feedback. Support touch, keyboard, and pointer input without making essential actions hover-only. Support text scaling and staff with varied technical and English proficiency.

## Redesign Direction

The UI overhaul may replace existing screen structures, navigation arrangements, and visual components from scratch where they obstruct the workflow. Existing layouts are not acceptance criteria. Preserve capabilities, access control, data integrity, and useful user context when changing the presentation.

[DESIGN.md](DESIGN.md) defines design guidance and distinguishes the implemented baseline from future decisions. [The UI overhaul plan](docs/ui-overhaul.md) records the screen sequence, initial findings, and visual review process. The first pilot is Patients with the shared application shell on mobile and desktop.
